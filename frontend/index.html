Vision Transformer (ViT) Deep Learning Architecture

01
Image Acquisition
High-res 256×1600 surface scan of cold-rolled steel feedstock sheets.
02
Patch Embedding
Linear projection of 16×16 patches into 384-dimensional token representations.
03
Transformer Encoder
6-layer Multi-Head Self-Attention capturing long-range spatial dependencies.
04
Attention Rollout
Recursive [CLS] attention propagation generating 2D anomaly heatmap.
05
Industry 4.0 Decision
Multi-class classification, RLE mask localization, and Grade A/B/C automated verdict.

Defect Classes Taxonomy (Severstal Standard)

Class 1: Pitted Surfaces

Pinhole cavities and inclusions from non-metallic impurities or slag entrapment in casting.

Class 2: Longitudinal Scratches

Directional lines from mechanical abrasion against mill guides or unpolished rolls.

Class 3: Scale & Crevices

Severe oxide scale crusts and thermal cooling fissures requiring acid wash pickling.

Class 4: Severe Gouges

Heavy plastic indentations and edge tearing requiring immediate shearing and quarantine.
